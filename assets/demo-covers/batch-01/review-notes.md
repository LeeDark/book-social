# Candidate Artwork Review

All 104 images were initially inspected on thumbnail contact sheets. Titles and
authors are not embedded in the images; the application displays them separately.

- `mathilda`: the first illustration resembled a children's scene with floating
  books and did not clearly match the work's serious subject.
- `the-wealth-of-nations`: the first illustration's classical figure and
  architecture did not clearly relate to Adam Smith's work.
- `the-way-to-wealth`: the first pastoral scene did not clearly convey the subject.
- Silhouettes against landscapes, sunset colours, and compositions recur across
  the collection.

## Visual review

On 2026-10-31 the user delegated review and acceptance of all 104 covers to Codex.
Codex inspected each selected image on contact sheets at 300×450 and reviewed the
thumbnail overview. All 104 were accepted as demo catalog illustrations: v2 was
selected for the three works above and the initial candidate for the others.
The [tracker](../catalog-tracker.md) records the decisions and the
[gallery](review.html) displays the selected versions. This is Codex's assessment
at the user's request, not individual approval by the user. The repeated style and
some illustrative rather than literal scenes remain. Application card and detail
page checks, installation, and release acceptance are still outstanding.

## Version 2

The three selected variants are in `data/demo-covers/batch-01/revisions/`;
the original versions are preserved. The gallery shows the selected variants.
Files have suffixes `-v2.png`, `-v2-main.webp`, and `-v2-thumb.webp`.
They were created with the built-in image generation tool; the model was not
exposed and no external references were used. Prompts:

- `mathilda`: Original project demo literary cover illustration: A sombre nineteenth-century English country house viewed across a rain-soaked garden, a solitary adult woman seen from behind beneath bare trees, quiet grief and isolation, no child, no books floating. Painterly gouache, engraved linework, paper texture, atmospheric lighting, clear shapes, portrait 2:3 full bleed. No lettering, logos, watermark, book mockup, or copied edition.
- `the-wealth-of-nations`: Original project demo literary cover illustration: An eighteenth-century Scottish port and bustling marketplace with merchants exchanging goods and ships in harbour, alluding to trade and division of labour. Painterly gouache, engraved linework, paper texture, atmospheric lighting, clear shapes, portrait 2:3 full bleed. No lettering, logos, watermark, book mockup, or copied edition.
- `the-way-to-wealth`: Original project demo literary cover illustration: An eighteenth-century Philadelphia print shop, a modest open ledger, coins and a printing press, visual metaphor for thrift and industriousness, no readable writing. Painterly gouache, engraved linework, paper texture, atmospheric lighting, clear shapes, portrait 2:3 full bleed. No lettering, logos, watermark, book mockup, or copied edition.
